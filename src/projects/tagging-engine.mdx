takes a video or image, detects fashion items with grounding dino, cuts them out with sam 2, drops near-duplicates with faiss, then embeds them with clip and searches a qdrant catalog for similar products. gemini adds a transcript, descriptions and a read on the overall vibe. served as a fastapi app.
