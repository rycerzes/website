built for reflection and hugging face's _build with beam_ contest. every other entry measured beam-1.0 (a 501b mixture-of-experts) from the outside, with benchmarks, effort sweeps and quantizations. i wanted to open it up and ask what each of its 12,544 routed experts actually does.

## how

i wrote **beam-steer**, a vllm plugin that can add a steering direction to the residual stream at a chosen layer, and ban exact experts by lowering their routing bias. then every expert got four tests:

1. **push** its top write direction into the running model and check the output drifts toward a theme more than random controls do
2. **label:** a blind judge matches that theme to the expert's top tokens
3. **self-label:** beam names the expert itself
4. **ban** every expert with a given label and see what beam can no longer answer

## what came out

- **2,092** expert directions steer beam beyond matched random controls, mostly in layers 24–39. given their top tokens, the blind judge picks the right output 70% of the time (chance is 33%).
- banning the 143 experts beam labels _law_ costs **13.4 points** on mmlu-pro law and nothing on any other subject.
- reasoning effort 32 matches effort 512 on aime/amc with 21% less thinking.

the full writeup has the method and figures, and the explorer lets you browse every expert.
